What changed

Each number matches a red marker on the page.

  1. 1
    "Price in about 20 seconds, no sign-up" up front. The thing that breaks the hesitation is now the headline.
  2. 2
    The sheet kit loads all 4 parts. Sheet parts start at 2 mm with a note, instead of a grey "fill in the specifications" button.
  3. 3
    Every finish keeps a demo price. Black anodizing shows a price marked "estimate". "0.00" never appears.
  4. 4
    Live price, no Apply button. Quick picks for 1, 10, 50, 100 pieces and a price table for each quantity.
  5. 5
    Express delivery. Pay more, get it sooner. Total and delivery time change straight away.
  6. 6
    Installments shown. Under 100,000 EGP a bar shows how much more unlocks them. Above it, they are available.
  7. 7
    Currency switch. Picked from the visitor's time zone, using live exchange rates.
  8. 8
    "Email me this quote". The PDF button and extra parts ask for an email only, so every interested visitor lands in our list.
  9. 9
    Full sign-up only at "Request quote". Value first, small ask next, big ask last (lazy registration). The email is already filled in.
  10. 10
    SolidWorks and other design files get help, not a dead end. entag.co lists these files, but the platform only takes 7 types. Now the customer sees how to save as STEP in a few clicks, or can send the file as it is for our engineers to price.
  11. 11
    Honest total. A part waiting for our engineers is named, and the total says "Total so far (1 of 2 parts)". Before, the screen showed a total that quietly left the part out. Only the PDF said so.
  12. 12
    No "go to your inbox" step. The request goes through right away. The customer confirms their email later, so they never leave the page in the middle.
  13. 13
    Clear next steps after "Request quote". Who checks it, by when, and where the answer arrives (email and WhatsApp). Channels and timings here are examples for Omar to confirm.
